What is Soothe?

Soothe is a crisis-intervention and anxiety relief app for iOS. When anxiety, panic, or overwhelming stress hits, Soothe guides you through proven calming techniques with step-by-step audio narration — no therapist appointment needed, no internet required.

Built on evidence-based practices used by mental health professionals, each exercise is designed to activate your body's natural calming response and bring you back to a grounded state.

Guided Calming Tools

A library of techniques, each professionally narrated and ready in seconds:

  • Paced Breathing — Slow, rhythmic breathing patterns
  • Grounding (5-4-3-2-1) — Sensory awareness exercises
  • Progressive Muscle Relaxation — Tension and release sequences
  • Cold Splash — Dive reflex activation technique
  • Distanced Self-Talk — Reframe anxious thoughts aloud
  • Yoga Nidra — Guided body-scan meditation
  • Gratitude Meditation — Shift focus to the positive
  • Crisis Flow — Rapid-access guided path for acute distress

Features

Professional Voice Narration

Every exercise is guided by clear, calming voice narration — choose from multiple voice options to find the one that works for you.

Works Offline

All core exercises work without an internet connection. Relief is always available, even in airplane mode.

Apple Health Integration

Automatically log Mindful Minutes and mood check-ins to Apple Health after each session.

Siri & Shortcuts

Launch your favorite exercise with a voice command or add it to your Shortcuts automations.

Personalized Recommendations

Soothe learns which tools you use most and surfaces recommendations tailored to your habits.

Customizable Experience

Choose your voice, font theme, color theme, haptic intensity, and countdown pace to make it yours.

Privacy First. Always.
