Soothe: Anxiety & Panic Relief

Guided Calm When You Need It

What is Soothe?

When anxiety hits, you need something that works — not a login screen, not a subscription wall, not a chatbot. Soothe puts seven clinically grounded calming techniques in your hands, each guided by professional voice narration, ready the moment you need them.

Whether you're managing a panic attack, spiraling thoughts, or just a rough day, Soothe walks you through each exercise step by step with clear audio guidance and calming visuals. No guesswork. No account required. It works offline, and your data never leaves your device.

Guided Calming Tools

A library of techniques, each professionally narrated and ready in seconds:

  • Paced Breathing — Seven patterns including 4-7-8, Box Breathing, and Physiological Sigh with animated visual guidance
  • 5-4-3-2-1 Grounding — Sensory awareness that interrupts spiraling thoughts. Full and quick (3-2-1) variants
  • Progressive Muscle Relaxation — Tension-and-release sequences in short, medium, or full-body sessions
  • Cold Splash — Activates the dive reflex to rapidly lower heart rate and calm your nervous system
  • Distanced Self-Talk — Cognitive reframing by speaking in the third person to reduce emotional reactivity
  • Yoga Nidra — Guided body-scan meditation for deep relaxation and sleep
  • Gratitude Meditation — Guided reflection that shifts focus from anxiety toward appreciation

Features

Audio Narration with Word Highlighting

Every exercise is guided by professional voice narration with karaoke-style word highlighting — follow along with your eyes or ears.

Crisis SOS Mode

A curated flow of exercises for acute distress, with quick access to emergency resources including the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.

Live Activities

Track your exercise progress on your Lock Screen and Dynamic Island so you can follow along even when you switch apps.

Works Offline

All core exercises work without an internet connection. Relief is always available, even in airplane mode.

Apple Health Integration

Automatically log Mindful Minutes and State of Mind mood entries to Apple Health after each session.

Siri & Shortcuts

Launch any exercise with a voice command or add it to your Shortcuts automations.

Background Ambient Music

Deepen relaxation with ambient music that continues playing even when your screen is locked.

Customizable Experience

Choose your narration voice, font theme, color theme, haptic intensity, and countdown pace to make it yours.

Privacy First. Always.

Soothe was designed with a privacy-first architecture. No accounts. No logins. No tracking. Your personal data never leaves your device.
