## 2. Description of Service

WaterBunny is an iOS application that helps you log daily water intake, track progress toward a hydration goal, and review your hydration history over time. The App offers the following features:

- Daily water logging with animated progress visualization
- Apple HealthKit integration for dietary water, caffeine, and electrolyte data
- Hydration reminders via local notifications
- Caffeine tracking with preset drink options
- Electrolyte tracking and product library (Pro feature)
- Historical insights and streak tracking
- Siri & Shortcuts integration
- Optional premium features available via one-time in-app purchase

## 6. HealthKit Data

### 6.1 — Scope of Access

With your explicit permission, WaterBunny reads and writes the following Apple HealthKit data types:

- **Dietary Water** — water intake logged in the app
- **Dietary Caffeine** — caffeine intake when caffeinated drinks are logged
- **Dietary Sodium, Potassium, Magnesium, and Chloride** — mineral data when electrolyte products are logged

### 6.2 — User Control

HealthKit access requires your explicit authorization through Apple's standard permission prompt. You may grant or revoke HealthKit permissions at any time through your device's Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices.

### 6.3 — Data Handling

In compliance with Apple's HealthKit guidelines and App Store Review Guidelines:

- HealthKit data is never transferred to external servers, cloud services, or third parties
- HealthKit data is never used for advertising, data mining, or marketing purposes
- HealthKit data is never sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party
- HealthKit data is governed by Apple's HealthKit terms and your device's privacy settings

### 6.4 — Data Persistence

HealthKit data written by WaterBunny persists within Apple Health independently of the App. Uninstalling WaterBunny does not delete data previously written to Apple Health. To manage or delete HealthKit entries, use the Apple Health app.
